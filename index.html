Corpus Linguistics & Lexicography

References, Corpora & Methodology

All high-frequency word lists and English loanword inventories in HiF-Lang are compiled and cross-verified against established open-access spoken and written linguistic corpora. Every link below points to an active, verified repository or linguistic institution.

1. Cross-Linguistic Spoken & Subtitle Frequency Corpora

Primary Spoken Proxy

OpenSubtitles & OPUS Parallel Corpus

Compiled by Jörg Tiedemann (University of Helsinki). Subtitle corpora closely mirror natural conversational word frequency across European languages.

opus.nlpl.eu ↗ github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords ↗
Written & Web Corpora

Leipzig Corpora Collection & Wiktionary

Large-scale newspaper, web, and Wikipedia word frequency lists maintained by Leipzig University and Wikimedia contributors.

corpora.uni-leipzig.de ↗ en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Frequency_lists ↗

2. Language-Specific National Academies & Lexical Databases

Spanish (Español) RAE • CREA / CORPES XXI

Real Academia Española — Corpus de Referencia del Español Actual (CREA) and Corpus del Español del Siglo XXI, alongside the Diccionario panhispánico de dudas for adapted and unadapted English loanwords.

rae.es ↗
French (Français) Lexique 3.83 • Académie française

Lexique 3 open psycholinguistic database (Boris New & Christophe Pallier) combining film subtitles and literary corpora, plus Éduscol / Académie française documentation on modern anglicisms.

lexique.org ↗
German (Deutsch) DeReKo • IDS Mannheim & DWDS

Deutsches Referenzkorpus (DeReKo) at the Leibniz-Institut für Deutsche Sprache (IDS Mannheim) and the Digitales Wörterbuch der deutschen Sprache (DWDS) frequency scales.

ids-mannheim.de ↗ dwds.de ↗
Italian (Italiano) NVdB (De Mauro) • Accademia della Crusca

Tullio De Mauro's Nuovo vocabolario di base della lingua italiana (NVdB), the CoLFIS lexical database, and the Accademia della Crusca observatory on unadapted English loanwords (forestierismi).

accademiadellacrusca.it ↗
Portuguese (Português) Corpus do Português • Linguateca

Mark Davies' Corpus do Português and the CETEMPúblico / Linguateca computational project covering both European and Brazilian Portuguese frequency distributions.

corpusdoportugues.org ↗ linguateca.pt ↗

3. Native Browser Speech Synthesis (W3C Web Speech API)

Audio pronunciation uses the native window.speechSynthesis interface. HiF-Lang automatically queries installed voices on your device and prioritizes neural/natural voices (Natural, Online, Google, Premium, Enhanced) matching the target BCP-47 locale tag (es-ES, fr-FR, de-DE, it-IT, pt-BR).

developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechSynthesis ↗
